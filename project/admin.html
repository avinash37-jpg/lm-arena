[Go] Publish Your Website

Step 1 — Download the updated data file

Clicking below will download a new data.js file containing all your edits, images, and settings.

Step 2 — Upload to Hostinger

  1. Log in to Hostinger hPanel.
  2. Open Files → File Manager.
  3. Navigate to your website's root folder (public_html).
  4. Open the js/ folder.
  5. Upload the downloaded data.js, replacing/overwriting the existing one.
  6. Open your website in an incognito tab to confirm changes are live.

Step 3 — Clear local draft

After publishing, click below to clear your browser's draft so future edits start from the live version.

! Backup reminder: Before uploading, you may want to download the existing data.js from Hostinger as a backup.
